01 — ORDER CARD
The card is the whole product
Every field on the card face maps to one property of MerchantOrderSummary. Reading order is number → status → customer → total → time → action, so a merchant scanning a column top-to-bottom answers "which one, how urgent, what do I press" in that order.

Empty, reconnecting, loading, error
All four keep the board frame and the column headers in place, so the merchant never loses the sense of where they are. Only the column bodies change.

The tray bar is always present at 64px, never auto-hides, and always shows its count — the third column stays discoverable without a gesture.
Expanded, it covers roughly half the viewport as a horizontally scrolling row and can be dismissed by tapping the bar, the scrim, or any card.
It auto-expands once when an order first enters READY_FOR_PICKUP, then collapses after 5 seconds.
Below 768px the board falls back to a single column with a segmented control across the three states. No separate mobile merchant workflow exists.
