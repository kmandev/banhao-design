M-12 — OPENING HOURS
BANHAO | บ้านเฮา · design artifact for docs/design/ · design only, no implementation
Screen M-12 เวลาเปิด–ปิดร้าน — the weekly schedule stored in restaurant_hours. Read against the deployed catalog schema, the catalog RLS policies and the merchant web app at ac2c88c5 / bc6e468e. The table's own comment sets the shape of this screen: “Replaced wholesale on edit — the application deletes and re-inserts a restaurant's rows.” One form, one save, the whole week. In-product copy is Thai, all annotation is English.
Product boundary, held. Opening hours are not order acceptance and not restaurants.status. 20260811000002 records that the open/closed a customer sees is derived by the application from status, hours and temporarily_closed_until together. This screen edits exactly one of those three inputs and introduces no control over the other two.

restaurant_hours holds four meaningful columns — day_of_week, opens_at, closes_at and the restaurant. There is no per-day “closed” flag: a closed day is the absence of a row. That single fact determines most of this design.

02 — STATE A · WEEKLY SCHEDULE
Desktop · ≥1024px · one row per day, always seven rows
Seven rows are always rendered, closed days included, because a merchant reads this screen to check a week — not to count the days that happen to have rows. Today's row is marked, since the most common reason to open this screen is “are we meant to be open right now”. The times are wall-clock Asia/Bangkok in 24-hour form, matching the time columns exactly, with no timezone label and no conversion.

03 — EDITING
The whole week is one form, because the write is one replacement
The table comment is unambiguous: an edit deletes and re-inserts the restaurant's rows. A per-day save button would therefore be a lie — pressing it would rewrite all seven days. So the screen is directly editable, tracks one dirty state for the week, and commits once. Nothing is saved until บันทึกเวลาทำการ is pressed.

Two rows for one day_of_week — explicitly allowed: “a shop that closes in the afternoon is representable”. The afternoon gap needs no representation of its own; a customer is simply outside every interval. This is why the day row holds a list of intervals rather than a single pair.
05 — LOADING · SAVING · SUCCESS · FAILURE
A destructive write deserves an honest failure state
Because the write is delete-then-insert, a failure mid-write is the one case that could leave a restaurant with no hours at all. The failure copy therefore never claims the previous schedule survived; it tells the merchant to check the week and try again, and the form keeps everything they entered.

06 — RESPONSIVE
Below 768px each day becomes a card, and the save bar sticks
Seven days do not fit a phone as a table. Each day becomes a card with the switch at the top and its intervals beneath, and the save bar pins to the bottom of the viewport — a one-save form is unusable if the button is seven cards down.
