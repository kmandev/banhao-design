01 — DESIGN RATIONALE
A recommendation and an authorization look identical on screen unless the design works to separate them.
The failure mode is not that a supervisor approves the wrong thing. It is that the interface makes approving feel like acknowledging — a card that says what the AI decided, and one button that agrees. Five rules keep the two apart, and each one costs the supervisor something: a read, a click, a typed reason. That cost is the design.

Stage 5 is the stage that does not exist in a normal approval UI. The AI wrote its recommendation at some earlier moment; the supervisor reads it now. Between those two moments the merchant may have accepted an order, been paused by someone else, or gone INACTIVE on their own. Stage 5 re-reads the domain immediately before the write and shows the supervisor what changed — and the approve control is bound to that read, not to the case.
03 — AUTHORIZATION MODEL · NO NEW ROLE
"Supervisor" is a job, not a role. The role is OPERATOR or ADMIN.
The deployed model is membership-based: platform_staff → capability resolution → route guard. DEC-033 is implemented with zero profiles.role references in any policy, and Phase B shipped the guards that way. This design adds no role, no scope, no temporary grant and no capability string that does not already follow from that chain.

06 — POLICY PANEL & EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
An undefined threshold is a visible fact on the screen, not an empty field.
Six values govern this command and none of them exists. Rendering them as blanks would read as "not loaded"; rendering them as zero would be a fabrication. They render as their decision id, in the colour the design reserves for blocked, and the panel states plainly which of them the approve control is waiting on.

09 — CONCURRENCY: TWO SUPERVISORS, ONE CASE
Both may open it. Only one may resolve it. The second must not be told they succeeded.
No locking, no soft claim that expires wrongly, no "B is editing" banner that lies when B closes their laptop. The case carries a version; resolution is a guarded conditional update whose WHERE clause carries the version B read. B loses the race and is told exactly that — ADR-003's rule applied to a UI, not just a table.

10 — ABSENT, NOT DISABLED
The controls this screen does not have, and why each absence is the design.

AI-01, unchanged and still open. audit_logs.actor_type is CHECK-constrained to CUSTOMER · MERCHANT · RIDER · OPERATOR · SYSTEM · WEBHOOK. There is no AI value, so an agent-authored escalation is indistinguishable from a scheduled tick in the audit table, and this flow's timeline can only label it "AI" by reading the case projection alongside the log. So the audit requirement in this brief is satisfiable today; only the AI attribution half is not. Widening the CHECK additively is one option and the convention "AI writes SYSTEM plus a discriminator in the payload" is the other. This package does not choose, and does not propose a migration.
