Four agents initially failed on a session limit and were relaunched successfully.

## Findings

Three findings changed the project's understanding of its own design:

1. **No payment provider examined supports native PromptPay refunds** (Omise: "cannot be voided or refunded"; Beam excludes it; Xendit's matrix marks it unsupported; Stripe requires emailing the customer for bank details). This **contradicts** the refund state machine in `docs/04-payment` and the automatic-refund-before-`PREPARING` rule in `docs/05-architecture`.
2. **The payment-facilitation licensing boundary is unresolved.** BANHAO's own design — platform calculates splits, runs transfer rounds, holds driver cash as a liability — may constitute regulated activity under the Payment Systems Act even when routing through a licensed PSP. Not resolvable from public sources.
3. **AWS (`ap-southeast-7`, Jan 2025) and GCP (`asia-southeast3`, Jan 2026) both now have Bangkok regions, both cheaper than Singapore** — correcting a premise given to the research agent. Latency, PDPA residency, and cost align rather than trade off.

Also: **Stripe disqualified** (TH Connect blocks separate charges and transfers; Food and Transportation both restricted). **HERE disqualified** (Base Plan licence excludes asset tracking). **ETDA Digital Platform Decree** may require notification even at Stage 1. **Rural Buntharik map accuracy is unmeasurable by desk research.**
